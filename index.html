Face & posture technique navigator

An organized guide to the techniques Oscar Patel covers on YouTube, with a practice outline for each.

Read first. These outlines are general summaries written from third-party video digests, not from the videos themselves, and they are not medical advice. The claims behind many of these techniques (changing bone shape or teeth alignment in adults) are not established by clinical research. Stop if you feel pain, numbness, jaw clicking or tooth soreness, and see a dentist or doctor before pressing on your palate or teeth.
